The problem

The old way of hosting now ends in "marriage poverty".

Hosts feel pressure to feed 300 to 1,000+ guests and have no way to recover the cost. Many couples start married life with an empty account or a high-interest loan. Three things have made it worse.

₦12,000

The Jollof Index

A wedding plate that cost ₦3,500 in 2022 now costs up to ₦12,000. That single plate is 17% of the ₦70,000 monthly minimum wage.

The gate-crashing tax

Up to 35% of the crowd at an open-invitation event was never invited. Feeding them costs a host ₦2.5 million per event on average, and invited guests go without seats or food.

InvitedUninvited

No legal way to spray

Spraying naira at parties used to cover the bill. The CBN and EFCC now treat it as currency abuse and prosecute it. Hosts need a legal, digital way for guests to give.

Sources: CBN Act §21 circular (2024) · EFCC public advisory (2024)
The solution

One platform. Fund it, control it, pay for it.

Planners help you spend money, registries collect gifts and ticketing apps scan tickets. Party Riders joins all three, so the money guests give is also what lets them in.

Fund

Experience Pass Portal

Your event gets its own branded page. Guests buy Silver, Gold or VIP passes and give gifts there, so the money comes in before you spend it.

  • Tiered passes, priced from Jollof Index data

  • Diaspora family can give in their own currency

Control

QR Digital Bouncer

Every guest gets a QR token that can't be passed on. When the gate scans it, the guest is given a meal and a seat. People without a pass don't get in.

  • Scanning keeps working when the network drops
  • Every plate is matched to a guest
  • Optional trained ushers at the gate

Liquidate

Flash-Pay Engine

Pay vetted vendors as soon as the money arrives. You lock in today's catering price months before the event, before inflation moves it.

  • Funds settle within 24 hours
  • Funds held in escrow with a Tier-1 bank
  • Money left over goes to your savings

No pass, no entry.  Every pass guests buy funds the party and is also their ticket in. Gifts go to the couple, not to people who weren't invited.

How it works

From budget to surplus in six steps.

  1. Host

    Set your goal

    Enter your budget and guest count. We suggest pass prices from current Jollof Index catering costs.

  2. Platform

    Get your event page

    Your branded page has a gift registry, payment and Aso-Ebi orders built in.

  3. Guests

    Share on WhatsApp

    Guests open the invite and buy passes. You watch the money come in, live.

  4. Host

    Pay vendors early

    Use Flash-Pay to book vendors as money arrives, so their prices are fixed.

  5. Gate

    Scan in on the day

    Ushers scan each QR token at the door, which gives that guest their seat and meal. Nobody gets in without a pass.

  6. Couple

    Keep the surplus

    After the event, anything left over goes into the couple's savings account.
